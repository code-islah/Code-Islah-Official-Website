Visual Representation

What These Properties Mean:

  • clientWidth/Height: Inner content area including padding but excluding scrollbar
  • clientTop/Left: Width of top/left borders (border width)
  • offsetWidth/Height: Element's visible dimensions including padding, border and scrollbar
  • offsetTop/Left: Distance from closest positioned ancestor
  • scrollWidth/Height: Total scrollable content size
  • scrollTop/Left: How much content has been scrolled
  • getBoundingClientRect(): Returns position relative to viewport
