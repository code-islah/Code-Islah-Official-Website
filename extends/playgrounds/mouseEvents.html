What These Coordinates Mean:

  • screenX/Y: Position relative to the entire screen
  • pageX/Y: Position relative to the entire document (including scrolling)
  • clientX/Y: Position relative to the viewport (excluding scrolling)
  • offsetX/Y: Position relative to the target element's padding edge
  • movementX/Y: Position change since the last mousemove event
  • layerX/Y: Position relative to the current layer (Mozilla-based browsers)

Move your mouse around the interactive area and click to see these values change. The red dot shows your pointer position, and click locations will be marked with smaller red dots.
